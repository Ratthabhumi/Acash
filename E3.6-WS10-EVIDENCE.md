## 6. Storage & Physical Risk Limitations

### Same-Disk Backup Warning (Risk R4)
On the HomeLab host, `/data/docker` (persistent state) and `/data/backups` (backup target) reside on the **same physical storage device**.
- **Assessment:** Local backup protects against accidental file deletion, corrupted state, bad container deployments, or human administrative error.
- **Limitation:** It does **NOT** protect against physical disk failure, server loss, or catastrophic storage degradation.
- **Requirement:** Off-host, encrypted incremental backup (Sprint 15) is required before true disaster recovery can be claimed.
