## Focused factual cautions

- `end` **inclusive is a documentation fact**, but it does not show that `end=2025-12-31` includes a NY-local 2025-12-31 daily bar after date-only parsing. Freeze a precise timestamp convention only after contract review and a separately authorized bounded probe or provider clarification. Do not silently extend the authorized calendar.
- A ten-year rolling display in October 2026 can omit part of early 2016; **cannot conclude its actual earliest row without inspecting export contents**. The latest rows in a page are not completeness evidence.
- Official public SEC files for GLD and GLDM must be keyed by ticker **and** SEC CIK/CUSIP. GLDM had a 2022 reverse split; **no comparable official GLD full-period split census has been established here**.
- The SPDR GLD historical XLSX may be pricing/NAV data. No claim of split support until contents legitimately inspected. GLD gold sold for expenses is not a cash dividend.
- `SOURCE_BYTES_RECONCILED` only authenticates internal consistency with supplied bytes; it does not establish issuer provenance, complete negative-event history, PIT vintage, independent review, or market-data rights.
