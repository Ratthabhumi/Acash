## 3–27. TWENTY-FIVE DIMENSION REVIEW

### 3. Mechanism

Precise causal chain (definitions fixed; truth UNPROVEN): [FROZEN definition / P — mechanism not
evidence]

1. **Event cause:** index-tracking funds and ETFs must trade constituent additions/deletions at a
   published **effective date** to minimize tracking error. The binding constraint is a timestamp,
   not a price. [R/I — hypothesis, not measured fact]
2. **Why forced:** index methodology mandates the change at the effective close for a fixed,
   pre-announced schedule. [R/I]
3. **Why price-insensitive:** demand (additions) / supply (deletions) must be completed near the
   effective close regardless of price level. [R/I]
4. **Temporary price pressure:** forced prints concentrate near the effective close, creating a
   transitory dislocation. [R/I — NP until measured]
5. **Post-event reversal:** once forced flow clears, price partially reverts; reversal bounded by
   shorting frictions on the deletion leg. [R/I — NP until measured]
6. **Falsification of the mechanism (pre-specified):** pre-registered tests (dimension 25) showing
   no economically meaningful post-cost net edge, or instability across pre-registered horizons /
   sub-periods, falsify the candidate. [FROZEN — kill conditions]

No vague terms are used without definition; "institutions buy" / "market overreacts" / "reversion
happens" are NOT substituted for the causal chain above. [V — discipline]
