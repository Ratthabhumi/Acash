## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemic Foundations

Phase 14 establishes the **ACASH AI Quantitative Research & Evidence Layer** (`acash.research.ai`). It transforms what is typically an undisciplined "LLM strategy generator" into an institutional, evidence-bound quantitative research workbench.

In accordance with the foundational ACASH research philosophy:
$$\boxed{\text{DO NOT ASSUME AN EDGE. PROVE IT.}}$$
and the core Strategy Admission epistemic identity (Phase 17 / ADR-023):
$$\boxed{\text{Observed Profit} \neq \text{Proven Skill} \neq \text{Structural Edge} \neq \text{Luck-Free Performance}}$$

Phase 14 strictly enforces the axiomatic identity:
$$\boxed{\text{AI Output} \equiv \text{Unvalidated Proposal} \quad (\text{Never Validated Alpha}, \ \text{Never Trading Authority})}$$

An LLM or machine learning assistant has **zero privileged access to market truth**. It holds no authority to register a hypothesis, execute an order, certify a backtest, validate statistical significance, or allocate capital. Every output generated by Phase 14 enters the existing ACASH research, backtesting, and statistical validation pipeline as an unverified candidate subject to strict fail-closed rejection.

## 4. Proposal vs. Trial Lifecycle & Multiple-Testing Governance

### 4.1 Strict Lifecycle Decoupling
To prevent invalid inflation or distortion of the multiple-testing penalty, Phase 14 strictly decouples the AI proposal lifecycle from the empirical trial lifecycle:

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│    - Status: UNVALIDATED_PROPOSAL                           │
│    - Pure symbolic / text proposal (Zero data access)       │

                               │

```

### 4.2 Authoritative $K$-Counting Rule
$$\boxed{\text{Phase 14 MUST reuse canonical Phase 6 / SearchTrialLedger semantics and MUST NOT invent a new } K \text{ definition.}}$$

1. **Rejected / Duplicate Proposals (Pre-Data):** If an `AIHypothesisProposal` is rejected during qualitative screening or AST parsing prior to data evaluation, **it does NOT increment $K$**.
2. **Exploratory Screening (Post-Data):** The instant a proposal evaluates prices/returns on historical data (even in VectorBT), every tested parameter permutation represents an empirical trial that **must be registered in `SearchTrialLedger` and included in $K$**.
3. **Census Preservation:** The trial count $K$ passed to Phase 6 `DeflatedSharpeCalculator` is derived strictly from `SearchTrialLedger.total_trials_count`, guaranteeing uncompromised multiple-testing governance.

## 5. Novelty & Duplication: Multi-Signal Heuristics

Rather than treating a single correlation threshold ($|\rho| > 0.85$) as mathematical proof of duplication, Revision 1.1 defines `DUPLICATE_CANDIDATE` as a multi-evidence hypothesis:

### Multi-Signal Evaluation Framework:
$$\text{DuplicationEvidence} = \left\langle \text{AST Similarity}, \ \text{Operator Grammar}, \ \text{Parameter Topology}, \ \text{Rank Correlation } \rho, \ \text{Residual Alpha } \alpha_{\text{incremental}} \right\rangle$$

1. **Rank Correlation ($\rho$):** Spearman rank correlation $|\rho| > 0.85$ triggers `DUPLICATE_CANDIDATE` status, initiating deep factor review.
2. **Residual Incremental Explanatory Power:** If regressing the proposed signal $S_{\text{new}}$ against known factors $F$ yields an incremental information coefficient $IC_{\text{incremental}} \approx 0$ ($t < 2.0$), the proposal is flagged as redundant.
3. **Operator Equivalence:** Normalizes symbolic expressions (e.g. `ema(x, 10) - ema(x, 50)` $\equiv$ `macd(x, 10, 50)`).
4. **Resolution Policy:** Flags as `DUPLICATE_CANDIDATE` with explicit lineage link to parent factor; requires researcher or sovereign justification to promote to registered trial.

## 6. Economic Mechanism Critic: Non-Blocking Exploratory Stance

The Economic Mechanism Critic enforces structured economic reasoning without becoming an absolute barrier to empirical inquiry:

$$\text{ECONOMIC\_REVIEW\_STATUS} \in \{\text{PLAUSIBLE}, \ \text{WEAK}, \ \text{REJECTED}, \ \text{UNKNOWN}\}$$

### Scrutiny & Gating Semantics:
- **`PLAUSIBLE`:** Clearly articulates market microstructure, behavioral bias, or structural constraint (e.g. order flow toxicity, liquidity provision discount).
- **`WEAK` or `UNKNOWN`:** Does **NOT** forbid empirical research or exploratory screening. Such hypotheses may proceed to exploratory backtesting with an explicit `EXPLORATORY_PHENOMENON` tag.
- **`REJECTED`:** Flagged if economic premise violates fundamental physical market laws (e.g. negative spread arbitrage, zero-latency assumptions).
- **The Non-Negotiable Gate Invariant:**
  $$\boxed{\text{Economic Plausibility} \neq \text{Statistical Validation} \neq \text{Alpha Qualification}}$$
  A strategy with `UNKNOWN` or `WEAK` economic rationale is strictly barred by Phase 8.5 `AlphaQualificationGate` from receiving a qualification dossier, regardless of empirical Sharpe ratio.

## 8. First-Class Negative Research Knowledge & Lineage

Failed research is among the most valuable assets of a quantitative fund. Phase 14 treats failed hypotheses as **first-class research artifacts** to prevent infinite rediscovery of fragile patterns.

### 8.1 Explicit Failure Taxonomies
Every rejected trial records a structured failure state:
$$\text{ResearchFailureState} \in \left\{
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{FAILED\_UNDER\_COSTS} & \text{(Alpha collapses under realistic spread/slippage)} \\
\text{FAILED\_UNDER\_OOS} & \text{(In-sample Sharpe $> 2.0$, OOS Sharpe $\le 0.0$)} \\
\text{FAILED\_UNDER\_REGIME} & \text{(Performance concentrated in single anomalous year)} \\
\text{FAILED\_CLIFF\_FRAGILITY} & \text{(Parameter surface cliff / narrow spike)} \\
\text{INVALIDATED\_BY\_DSR} & \text{(Fails multiple-testing selection penalty } K\text{)} \\
\text{NOT\_REPRODUCED} & \text{(External paper/claim fails reproduction on ACASH data)}
\end{array}
\right\}$$

### 8.2 Negative Semantic Graph Relations:
The Research Knowledge Graph explicitly incorporates negative edges:
- `INVALIDATED_BY`: Links hypothesis to the specific test that caused failure.
- `FAILED_UNDER`: Links strategy to the specific market regime or friction multiplier.
- `CONFOUNDED_BY`: Links apparent alpha to a common market factor (e.g. beta, size).
- `NOT_REPRODUCED`: Documents external literature claims that failed empirical validation.

## 12. Determinism, Provenance & Reproducibility Semantics

Revision 1.1 eliminates misleading assertions of "deterministic LLMs" and replaces them with a precise engineering specification:

1. **Deterministic Orchestration:** The Python workflow, prompt assembly, AST parsing, and state transitions are 100% deterministic and unit-tested with seed controls.
2. **Bounded Model Interaction:** Calls to external LLM providers pin temperature, max tokens, top-$p$, and random seed.
3. **Immutable Input/Output Lineage:** Because external providers do not guarantee bitwise determinism across model revisions or hardware clusters, reproducibility is guaranteed by recording the exact `input_context_sha256` and `raw_response_sha256` in the `ResearchManifest`.
4. **Offline Replayability:** Research replay testing operates against cached completions indexed by input hash, allowing bitwise-reproducible local execution without network calls.
